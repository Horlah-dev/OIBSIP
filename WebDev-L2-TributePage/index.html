His Story

About Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein was born on March 14, 1879, in Ulm, Germany. From an early age, he developed a strong curiosity about mathematics, science, and the way the natural world worked. His curiosity eventually led him toward a career that would transform modern physics.

In 1905, often described as his remarkable year, Einstein published several groundbreaking scientific papers. Among them was his work on special relativity, which changed the way scientists understood space, time, and motion. He also introduced the famous relationship between mass and energy, expressed as E = mc².

Einstein later developed his theory of general relativity, providing a new explanation of gravity and its relationship with space and time. His scientific contributions made him one of the most recognized physicists of the twentieth century.

In 1921, Einstein was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect. Beyond his scientific work, he became known around the world for his curiosity, independent thinking, and belief in the importance of imagination and education.
